About Us

Fest Globe Corp (FGC) is a modern, digital-first event services platform designed to simplify planning, booking, and management for individuals and businesses alike. Operating across **B2C (customer-facing)** and **B2B (partner-facing)** segments, we provide end-to-end solutions through our two flagship applications:

Our ecosystem emphasizes **transparency, reliability, and ease-of-use**, ensuring every event — big or small — is handled smoothly. Our Applications

FestGo (Customer App)

FestGo helps users discover and book event services based on their **PIN-code**. It offers instant booking, package comparison, and transparent pricing.

FestGoP (Partner App)

FestGoP allows service providers to manage listings, bookings, and pricing. Validation is based on **PIN-code + Mobile Number** for accuracy and authenticity.

Our Services

FGC offers a curated range of event services through trusted and verified partners:

  • Catering – Vegetarian, Non-Vegetarian & Customized Menus
  • Photography & Videography – Weddings, Parties, Corporate Events
  • Decoration – Stage, Theme Designs, Floral, LED & Backdrops
